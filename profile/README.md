*Innovating, building, and showcasing robotics and many more*

Welcome to the digital workshop for E-Yantra enthusiasts at AOT! We are a collective of passionate makers, coders, and hardware tinkerers dedicated to pushing the boundaries of embedded systems and robotics. 

###  What We Build
This organization serves as our collaborative portfolio. Here, we document our journey, open-source our code, and showcase the physical systems and algorithms we design for E-Yantra competitions and independent projects. 

###  Our Toolbox
*   **Hardware:** Microcontrollers, Custom PCBs, Sensors & Actuators
*   **Software:** C/C++, Python, ROS
*   **Focus:** Embedded Systems, Autonomous Robotics, Circuit Design

###  Explore & Connect
Feel free to poke around our repositories, fork what interests you, and drop a star on the projects you like.
